Process

A simple delivery loop designed to reduce rework.

  1. Scope: define inputs, outputs, deadline, and acceptance criteria.
  2. Build: produce the smallest working version first.
  3. QA: check data, logic, formatting, references, and edge cases.
  4. Handoff: deliver editable files, concise notes, and reproducible steps where relevant.
